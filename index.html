The principle

AI in support should do all the time-consuming, repetitive tasks so humans can remain the warm human element in the relationship.

I'm a senior customer support associate who's at my best when the case is hard — the ones that have already broken a process, a deadline, or a relationship. My goal is to move from resolving individual customer issues into the work that prevents them: customer experience operations.

That through-line runs through my whole career. I started in 911 dispatch, triaging emergencies under pressure, then carried that composure into insurance claims and mortgage processing — investigating files, enforcing standards, and coordinating across underwriters, closers, realtors, and vendors. I proved I could run operations when I lifted property occupancy from 78% to 96%, then retooled for tech: a software engineering fellowship, an open-source fellowship at Sauce Labs, and hands-on AI building for support.

At Zoro, working complex cases has clarified what I want next: the systems side of customer experience, where I help prevent repeat issues instead of only resolving them one at a time.
